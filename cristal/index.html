💎 CRISTAL Lab

Communication, Reasoning, Intelligence & Safety for Trustworthy Alignment

🚀 Open Positions in Trustworthy AI

We are recruiting fully funded PhD Students, Postdoctoral Researchers, and Interns at the CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security starting Fall 2026!

Welcome to the CRISTAL Lab at CISPA! Our mission is to develop cutting-edge AI systems that can communicate effectively, reason reliably, operate safely in complex environments, and make transparent and trustworthy decisions.

We are building a collaborative, ambitious, and inclusive research group focused on pushing the boundaries of AI alignment, reasoning, safety evaluation, and interpretability. If you are passionate about building the future of trustworthy AI, explore our open positions and application guidelines below.

🎯 Research Vision

Recent advances in foundation models have created powerful capabilities, but also raise fundamental challenges in safety, alignment, and reliability. CRISTAL focuses on three interconnected research directions:

  • AI Alignment: How can we build AI systems that behave in accordance with human intent and values while remaining robust and reliable?
  • Safety Evaluation: How can we systematically evaluate AI systems before deployment and anticipate failure modes in advance?
  • Transparent Decision-Making: How can we make modern AI systems more interpretable, accountable, and understandable?

Our goal is to develop principled methods for building AI systems that are both highly capable and fundamentally trustworthy.

📋 Application Materials

Applicants should submit the following materials:

  1. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  2. Research statement (2–3 pages)
  3. 2 representative research artifacts, such as:
    • Publications or preprints
    • Research or engineering projects
    • Code repositories or technical reports
  4. Transcript of records (Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD, as applicable)
  5. Contact details of three academic advisors willing to provide a reference letter upon request
  6. (Optional) Answers to Research Questions (see details below)

💡 Research Statement Guide

Applicants are encouraged to discuss both their past research contributions and their future research vision, including how their interests connect to CRISTAL's research themes. We evaluate research statements based on clarity, critical thinking, and alignment with our vision.

❓ Optional Research Questions (Highly Encouraged)

Applicants who wish to provide additional signal may respond to one or several of the following questions (1–2 pages per question). Responses are intended to showcase independent thinking and scientific creativity:

  • How would you evaluate whether a modern AI system behaves reliably and in accordance with intended goals?
  • What are important or underexplored failure modes in current state-of-the-art AI systems?
  • How would you design methods to assess or improve the transparency and interpretability of AI systems?
  • What limitations do you see in current approaches to evaluating AI safety and robustness?

📊 Evaluation Criteria

We evaluate applicants based on research potential appropriate to their career stage:

PhD Applicants:

  • Curiosity and motivation for research
  • Ability to think critically about scientific problems
  • Evidence of technical skills (projects, coursework, or early research)
  • Trajectory and growth potential

Postdoctoral Applicants:

  • Scientific independence and leadership potential
  • A coherent and independent research agenda
  • Demonstrated publication record in relevant venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, COLM, etc.)
  • Ability to define and lead new research directions
  • Strong alignment with CRISTAL’s research vision

🏛️ About the Lab, Director & CISPA

About the Director:
Dr. Cristina Garbacea is incoming Tenure-Track Faculty Member and Chief Scientist at CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security, and the founding director of the CRISTAL Lab. She completed postdoctoral research at the University of Chicago and obtained her PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Michigan. Her research spans trustworthy AI, foundation models, AI alignment, safety evaluation, reasoning, and transparency.

About the CRISTAL Lab:
The CRISTAL Lab combines advances in machine learning, NLP, reasoning, and AI safety to address foundational questions in alignment, evaluation, and trustworthy decision-making. As a newly established group, CRISTAL emphasizes close mentorship, collaborative research, intellectual independence, and professional development.

About CISPA:
CISPA is a leading global research institution in computer science and cybersecurity research, focusing on foundational and applied research in trustworthy systems, AI, and security. Researchers at CISPA regularly publish at top-tier international venues.
